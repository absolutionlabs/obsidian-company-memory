## 3. AI-generated outputs may contain errors

The skill installs a vault structure intended to be edited by an AI assistant (Cowork, Claude Code, Codex, opencode, or another LLM-powered tool). LLMs can and do produce inaccurate, incomplete, or fabricated outputs. They can misinterpret your SCHEMA, misclassify pages, hallucinate wikilinks, propose incorrect facts about your company, and generate content that appears authoritative but is wrong.

**You are solely responsible for reviewing AI-generated content before relying on it.** The lint operation built into the vault catches some categories of drift (orphan pages, broken wikilinks, stale content), but it cannot verify factual accuracy of vault contents. **Independently verify anything important** before acting on AI-generated outputs from this vault, especially:

- Facts about your company, customers, suppliers, contracts, or pricing
- Decisions logged in `log.md` or `index.md`
- Synthesised answers in the `queries/` folder
- Anything that could affect your finances, legal position, regulatory compliance, or contractual obligations

This applies to outputs generated by *any* AI tool reading from or writing to a vault scaffolded by this skill, regardless of model provider.

## 5. No guarantee of fitness for your specific use case

We have built the skill for a particular shape of user — founders and small-team operators of 2 to 50 people, who already use an AI assistant, and who want their AI's outputs to accumulate as durable company knowledge. The skill's design, defaults, scope, and deliberate cuts are calibrated for that audience.
