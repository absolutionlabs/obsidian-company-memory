## Working Backwards

When this is done, **Absolution Labs LTD** will have a **public, professionally-branded, single-URL-installable skill** that lets **prospective clients install an Obsidian-based company memory themselves in ~25 minutes and experience it as proof that Absolution Labs LTD ships trustworthy, working, secure tools** — without writing a line of code or touching a terminal. The skill is itself the sales pitch; every install is in front of a prospect; per-install quality is absolute.

- LICENSE (MIT) + comprehensive disclaimer set (all referencing "Absolution Labs LTD" — full legal name)

## Pre-Mortem

The skill shipped and an early prospect — exactly the kind of regulated-sector founder Absolution Labs LTD is positioned to sell to — installed it from the URL we sent them. The Cowork install half-succeeded, leaving a partial `.obsidian/` config and three orphaned markdown files in their Dropbox; or worse, the skill silently overwrote an existing `notes/` folder they'd had for years. Within a week they'd installed our broken artifact, lost data, written client information into a vault syncing to a Dropbox account that didn't carry the DPA their sector requires, and they associated all three failures with "the people who sent me this." The sales conversation that the skill was supposed to anchor was dead before it started, and the prospect (informally or formally) mentioned the experience to one or two peers in the same regulated network.
